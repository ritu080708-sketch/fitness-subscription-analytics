**Customer Cohort Analysis:** After how many months do customers typically cancel their subscriptions?
Answers: Customers typically cancel their subscriptions after 3 months. When looking horizontally across the rows under the "Months Since First Purchase" columns there is a distinct 
color shift and there is a sharp drop in active customer numbers across almost every single cohort starting at Month 3. In January 1 2024, active customers drop from 122 in Month 2 
to 78 in Month 3.  In February 1 2024, active customers drop from 130 in Month 2 to 84 in Month 3. In March 1 2024, active customers drop from 140 in Month 2 to 90 in Month 3.  In 
April 1 2024, active customers drop from 150 in Month 2 to 96 in Month 3. This consistent drop-off pattern indicates that Month 3 represents the primary churn point.

**CAC vs. LTV:** How long does it take for the 2024 and 2025 cohorts to recover their customer acquisition cost?
For the  2024 cohort, it takes 12 months to recover their customer acquisition cost (CAC).The Cumulative revenue (LTV) curve crosses their line between month 11 (841,650) 
and month 12 (893,655) meaning the acquistion cost is fully recovered by month 12.
For the 2025 cohort, it takes between 4 and 5 month to fully recover the customer acquisition cost. At month 4, the cumulative LTV is 1,054806 which is just below the CAC line. 
By month 5, the cumulative LTV rises to 1,140299 crossingabove the CAC line to achieve full payback.
